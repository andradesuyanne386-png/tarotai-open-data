# TarotAI Daily Reflection — Privacy Policy
Effective November 6, 2026.

This extension stores only your current daily card, its date and orientation, and a reflection note in chrome.storage.local on your device. Notes are saved only when you select Save note. Drawing on a new day replaces the previous entry. Clear local card & note removes this information; uninstalling the extension also removes its local storage.

The extension does not transmit this data to TarotAI or any other service. It has no analytics, advertising, login, cloud synchronization, content scripts, remote code or remote AI calls. It does not read your browsing history or website content. The storage permission is used only for the card and note.

Opening the optional TarotAI homepage link is a normal website visit; the website's separate privacy policy applies. The extension does not append your card or note to the link. Downloaded or manually copied information remains under your control.
